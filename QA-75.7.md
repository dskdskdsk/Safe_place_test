# 75.7 Status limits and editing

## Acceptance checks

- Three built-in statuses plus at most seven custom statuses are available.
- The eighth custom status cannot be created and a localized toast explains the limit.
- Saving or cancelling closes and clears the custom-status form.
- Selecting a custom status does not reveal the travel/details input.
- A double tap opens the compact name editor without adding a permanent edit icon.
- Renaming preserves the status ID and all day references.
- Visually identical statuses receive distinct UUID-based IDs and remain independent.
- Ukrainian and English labels are available.
