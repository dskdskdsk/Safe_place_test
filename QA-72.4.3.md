# 72.4.3 — four possible observations and readable evidence

Up to four slides: summary, habit/status association, emotion or missing coverage,
and consecutive-day changes when eligible. No fifth slot.

Swing eligibility is a product rule: at least five consecutive rated pairs in
each aligned period, at least 50% rating coverage in each, and a difference
of at least 0.3 points in mean absolute consecutive-day change. Missing dates
are never bridged. This is descriptive, not a claim of emotional stability.

“Behind these insights” now explains dates, rated days, daily weighting,
calendar comparison, consecutive pairs and limitations in prose. Full metrics
remain collapsed under “All metrics”. UK and EN text provided.

Tests: insight_d.py mobile matrix and synthetic swing/pair-count boundary.
The explanation is currently shared across the period; it does not yet provide
individual habit/status subgroup evidence for each slide.
